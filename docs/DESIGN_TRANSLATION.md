# Design Translation Sheet

- Brand personality: established, authoritative, personal, service-led, Embu-rooted and contemporary.
- Audience: individuals, families, employers, employees, property participants and organisations seeking legal assistance in Embu and Kenya.
- Main conversion action: discuss a matter by telephone or email.
- Palette: royal navy and deep ink as the main architecture; restrained gold rules and actions; red reserved for focus and micro-detail; white and soft neutral reading surfaces.
- Typography: editorial Georgia display type paired with a direct system sans-serif for navigation and body copy.
- Hero mode: asymmetric portrait-led split composition using Victor's official portrait, with an information stamp carrying the official logo.
- Practice presentation: full-width numbered index on dark navy, followed by detailed two-column editorial blocks on the practice page.
- Section sequence: portrait hero → verified trust strip → firm approach → practice index → real contextual photography → advocate authority → standards → guides → direct contact.
- Signature device: circular line geometry derived from the logo, fine gold rules, large editorial numbering and a portrait stamp.
- Photography: genuine client portrait for identity; real, licensed Kenya land and professional documentary imagery for property, disputes and workplace context.
- Differentiation from the source foundation: portrait-first hero instead of consultation scene; navy/gold institutional architecture instead of purple rights-led styling; horizontal indexed practices instead of image cards; serif-led typography; trust strip and gold contact band; no animated logo sequence; reordered home narrative.
