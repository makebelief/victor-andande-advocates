Employment guide

Termination: a practical starting point.

Organise the employment record and the sequence of events before drawing conclusions.

Questions about termination usually involve both the stated reason and the process followed. The documents and chronology matter.

Build a clear record

  • Employment contract and any later amendments
  • Relevant workplace policies or collective documents
  • Payslips, attendance records and leave records
  • Warnings, notices, invitations, responses and meeting notes
  • The termination letter and final payment information

Separate reason from process

A review may need to consider what reason was given, what information supported it, whether the employee had an opportunity to respond, and how the employer reached the decision. The applicable requirements depend on the facts and relationship.

Preserve communications

Keep relevant email, messages and letters in full. Avoid altering screenshots or discarding context. Make a dated chronology while events are still fresh.

Act within the relevant time

Employment disputes can be subject to statutory and procedural time limits. Prompt advice helps identify the correct forum, issues and next steps.

For employees and employers

The appropriate analysis depends on the contract, facts and process.
