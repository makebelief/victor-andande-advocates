Legal documentation guide

Deed polls and change of name.

A formal change requires accurate documents and attention to the steps for registration, publication and later record updates.

A deed poll records a person’s decision to abandon a former name and adopt a new one. The correct supporting documents and process depend on the applicant’s circumstances.

Prepare accurate identity records

Gather current identification, birth or registration records and any document relevant to the reason or history of the proposed name change. Differences in spelling or prior names should be raised early.

Draft and execute the instrument

The deed poll must identify the former and adopted names accurately and be completed in the legally required manner. Requirements can differ where a minor or another special circumstance is involved.

Registration and publication

Formal steps may include registration and publication of a notice. The order and documentary requirements should be confirmed for the particular application.

Update other records

A deed poll does not automatically update every record held by another institution. After the formal process, the person may need to approach relevant agencies, banks, schools, employers or other record holders with the required evidence.

Accuracy matters

Names and identity details should be checked carefully across every supporting document.
