01 · About the firm

Service with purpose.

Legal work is personal before it is procedural. We keep the client’s practical position in view while giving the law, evidence and next steps careful attention.

Our approach

A firm rooted in Embu and focused on the work.

Victor L. Andande & Co. Advocates is a legal practice based on Mama Ngina Street in Embu, Kenya.

The firm acts in matters that include land and property, civil disputes, employment and labour, succession and estate administration, and formal change-of-name work. Its public record reflects active appearances in Kenyan courts across several of these areas.

Our organising idea is simple: selfless service delivery. In practice, that means listening carefully, preparing diligently, explaining the legal position plainly and remaining accessible as a matter moves forward.

“A Law Firm focused on selfless service delivery.”
