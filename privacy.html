Privacy

Information handled with care.

This notice describes how information shared through this website may be handled.

Information you choose to provide

You may provide your name, email address, telephone number and a short account of your enquiry. The website’s enquiry form prepares an email in your own email application; the website does not store the form content in a database.

How information may be used

Information received by the firm may be used to respond to your enquiry, assess whether the firm can assist, communicate with you, administer an accepted instruction and meet applicable legal or professional obligations.

Confidentiality and sensitive information

Please do not send highly sensitive or confidential documents through an initial website enquiry unless the firm specifically requests them. Contacting the firm does not by itself create an advocate-client relationship.

Third-party services

This website is hosted by Vercel. If you use links to your email application, telephone provider or Google Maps, those third-party services process information under their own policies.

Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures should be used to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your questions

Privacy questions may be sent to andande.law@gmail.com or raised by calling +254 729 099307.

Website scope

This notice addresses the public website and initial contact path. It does not replace professional, statutory or regulatory obligations that may apply to an accepted legal instruction.
